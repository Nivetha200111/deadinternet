Reading the lens

Dead Internet Score = 40% automation-like account share + 20% coordinated account share + 25% near-duplicate reply share + 15% share of accounts in clusters of five or more. It is uncalibrated and is not the probability that a conversation is fake.

Automation and coordination are scored independently. People can coordinate; automated accounts can act alone.

Clusters are connected components of the coordination graph, computed in FalkorDB with algo.WCC. Coordination combines text similarity (55%), timing proximity (30%) and shared phrases (15%).

Sensitivity moves the classification thresholds over stored scores. It does not re-run the classifier.

Classifications describe behavioral, textual, timing and graph signals only. They are never proof of who, or what, is behind an account.
