How it works

  1. Open a single post on X or LinkedIn and scroll through its replies or comments, or press Auto-collect.
  2. Press Analyze thread. Replies are sent only to your local Lens server.
  3. Watch the graph build. Each reply on the page gets a badge: click one to inspect that account, or click a node in the graph to jump to its reply.

Classifications describe text, timing and graph signals in this thread. Account metadata isn't visible on the page, so it isn't used. Results are an experimental heuristic, never proof of who runs an account.
