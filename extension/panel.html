How it works

  1. On your X or LinkedIn feed, a profile or search results, scroll to collect the posts themselves. Or open a single post to collect its replies or comments. Auto-collect scrolls for you.
  2. Press Analyze posts (or Analyze thread). Text is sent only to your local Lens server.
  3. Watch the graph build. Each post or reply on the page gets a badge: click one to inspect that account, or click a node in the graph to jump to it.

Classifications describe text, timing and graph signals in what was collected. Account metadata isn't visible on the page, so it isn't used. Results are an experimental heuristic, never proof of who runs an account.
